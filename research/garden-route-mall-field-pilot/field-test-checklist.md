# Garden Route Mall — Sunday field-test checklist (route-specific)

Open on the phone: `/navigate?mall=garden-route-mall&start=grm-entrance-4`. Garden Route Mall and
Entrance 4 are preselected; tap **Woolworths** (Route A) or **Pick n Pay** (Route B). Clicks is a
third destination on the way to Pick n Pay. No equipment needed. Paces are **approximate**: count
double-steps per leg and write the count; do not convert to metres on site. Each leg is measured
**separately** — one leg never calibrates the rest of the map.

Before either route: read the shops page to confirm store numbers (Woolworths 9, Pick n Pay 41,
Clicks 37, Game 129, Dis-Chem 122/123, Food Lovers 131); read the contact page for the exact
"Security offices … Centre Management" sentence and whether it mentions Entrance 2; photograph the
printed directory board at Entrance 4, square-on, plus its legend.
