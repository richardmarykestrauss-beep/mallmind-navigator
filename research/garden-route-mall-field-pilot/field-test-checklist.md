# Garden Route Mall — Sunday field-test checklist

Print this or keep it open in Notes. No equipment needed beyond a phone. Paced distances are
**approximate** (count double-steps; note your stride ≈ 0.75 m for a single step if you want a rough
metre figure, but record the pace count itself). If your phone has a measuring app (e.g. iPhone
Measure) use it only for short checks such as corridor width; it is an aid, not survey truth.

## Before you walk
- [ ] Open the shops page on your phone; confirm the six store numbers (Clicks 37, Pick n Pay 41,
      Woolworths 9, Dis-Chem 122/123, Game 129, Food Lovers 131) and note any that differ.
- [ ] Read the contact page: does it say the Security / Centre Management offices are **near
      Entrance 2**? Record the exact sentence.
- [ ] Photograph the printed **"You are here" directory board** at your start entrance, square-on,
      full frame, plus a close-up of the legend / any scale bar / entrance numbers.
- [ ] Note the time, and the entrance number printed on the door or signage where you start.
